# Confirm bookings by quote acceptance

The customer's budget range guides WANAS's review. After checking availability, an admin sends a quote with the included services and one total in EGP. The quote may adjust the requested scope. WANAS keeps the original request for comparison. The customer can accept or decline. Acceptance confirms the booking without a payment step. WANAS can confirm one event per Cairo calendar day.

Multiple customers can request the same date. Sending a quote gives its recipient an exclusive 48-hour hold on the date. If the customer accepts, the hold becomes a booking. A decline or expiry releases the date. The hold lets the customer rely on the quoted date while deciding whether to accept.

The date picker shows holds and keeps those dates open for new requests. Once the date is booked, the admin inbox flags competing requests as Date unavailable so WANAS can offer another date or close them. Admins can replace an active quote or send a new one after expiry if the date is available. Each new quote has its own 48-hour window. The request history keeps previous quotes, and the customer can accept the current quote only.

New requests require 10 calendar days' notice. Customers can withdraw an unbooked request or contact WANAS to discuss changes. After confirmation, customers can request a change, cancellation, or reschedule, and admins apply agreed changes. Both actions must occur by the end of the Cairo calendar day 10 days before the event.

When the requested date is unavailable, WANAS first discusses an alternative with the customer. The quote shows the offered date and acceptance books that date. For changes to an existing booking, WANAS agrees on the scope and any price difference directly with the customer. Admins record the agreement alongside the original accepted quote.

The 10-day minimum also applies when an admin sends a quote for a different date or reschedules a booking. An admin can quote for the original requested date inside that window if the full 48-hour hold expires before the start of the event day in Cairo. This gives WANAS time to review requests submitted exactly 10 days ahead and gives every customer the full response window.
