# Event decor planning

WANAS plans event decor and offers catering for events in Cairo. Customers describe their event, and the WANAS team reviews the request and sends a quote in Egyptian pounds (EGP).

## Language

**Event request**:
A customer's submission for a specific event. It includes the event type, estimated guest count, requested date, venue, budget range, and planning mode.

**Requested date**:
The Cairo calendar date the customer asks WANAS to book. New requests require at least 10 calendar days' notice.

**Venue**:
The Cairo location the customer provides for the event, identified by its name, street address, and type, such as an apartment, villa, or garden.

**Budget range**:
The customer's total spending range in EGP for decor and any requested catering.

**Planning mode**:
The customer's choice to let WANAS select the decor or to choose decor options themselves.

**Let WANAS choose the decor**:
WANAS chooses the decor. The customer can share preferences or notes.

**Customize my decor**:
The customer chooses a moodboard, palette, and decor elements. They can leave individual choices to WANAS.

**Catering**:
An optional food and dining service the customer can request alongside decor in either planning mode.

**Quote**:
WANAS's offer for an event request, with the included services and one total price in EGP. The scope may differ from the customer's request.

**Offered date**:
The event date in a quote. It may differ from the requested date when the customer and WANAS discuss an alternative.

**Quote hold**:
A 48-hour reservation of an event date for the customer receiving the current quote. Other customers can still request the date during the hold.

**Booking**:
An event confirmed by the customer's acceptance of a WANAS quote.

**Booking change cutoff**:
The end of the Cairo calendar day 10 days before the booked event. Both the customer request and the admin action must occur by then.
